# Culture-pair statements (draft C roster)

Working sheet for soft-SF 4X diplomacy flavor. Each directed pair (Speaker → Target) may carry:


| Field             | Meaning                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Belligerent**   | Threat, insult, contempt, cold warning — in the speaker’s voice          |
| **Complimentary** | Praise, respect, fondness, or backhanded admiration — still in character |


**Blank (**`—`**)** = insufficient lore yet; do not invent under pressure. Fill when the histories support it.

Source tone: `conventional-species-history-c.md`. Includes select **abductee** polities where contact lore is already sharp.

---

## How to use

- Prefer **one sharp line** over a speech.  
- Belligerent ≠ always war; Complimentary ≠ alliance.  
- Abductee ↔ parent pairs often out-bite stranger pairs.  
- **Softnight Kin** outbound compliments name a **gentle pastime** of the target.  
- **Inbound to Softnight:** speakers allude to **their own cultural idyll** (“you live what we mean by enough”).
