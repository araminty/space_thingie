# Conventional species — histories (draft C)

Working draft for soft-SF 4X setting. **Conventional factions** only: tool-using civilizations that climb a suspiciously parallel ladder and, by 2400, sit at the door of hyperlane travel.

Human dating marks shared hinges. Local calendars differ; historians translate.

**Draft C** keeps draft B’s ~1400 and abductee rules, and revises **~1800**, **~2200**, and **2384**.

---

## The coincidence

Every conventional culture below hits the same four hinges within a few decades of the same human-calendar years:


| Human year (approx.) | Shared hinge                                                                  | What varies                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **~1400 CE**         | **Two breakthrough tools** answering urgent needs or conflicts                | Which needs; which tools                                                                     |
| **~1800**            | **One extremely disruptive technology** that overcomes a **major constraint** | What was bottlenecked; what breaks it                                                        |
| **~2200**            | **Life beyond “only the homeworld as-found”**                                 | New worlds in-system *or* reclaiming a shattered / abandoned homeworld at scale              |
| **2384**             | **A path to hyperlane / hyperspace travel**                                   | How they notice it — outlook, craft accident, lap-fall, or **innate gift unlocked/reframed** |


Before ~1400: a **plateau** (law, art, empires, specialist crafts evolve; baseline power/productivity mostly does not).

The synchrony is unexplained here.

## How ~1400 works

Name **two** significant **needs** or **conflicts** (environment, rivals, scarcity, external threats, legitimacy — not only civil war). Name tools invented ~1400 that show either:

1. **greater attention to practical concerns**, or
2. **increasingly sophisticated study of the world**.

Not every culture gets “powder + textile machine.”

### Human template

1. **Textiles to endure environments** — spinning wheel cuts fabric cost.
2. **Nation-state warfare** — gunpowder weapons prove highly effective in inter-human war.

---



## How ~1800 works

Not “must invent a chemical heat engine.”

Name a **major constraint** that had capped the culture (energy at point of use, memory, distance, trust, season, silence, symmetry, etc.). Name an **extremely disruptive** technology that overcomes it — mechanical, biological, informational, social-instrument, geological, etc.

### Human template

**Constraint:** Mechanical work limited to human/animal muscle fed by agriculture, or to specialized site-tied sources (sails, waterwheels) that demanded convenient energy at point of use.  
**Disruption:** The **expansion steam engine** — tools far beyond muscle, powered by chemical energy that can be **transported to need**.

---



## How ~2200 works

They no longer live only on the homeworld in its inherited state. Common patterns:

- Permanent settlements elsewhere in the **home system** (moons, belts, aerostats…).  
- **Inversion:** large-scale **reclamation** of a shattered, poisoned, drowned, or abandoned homeworld — “beyond” the world-as-lost, not necessarily beyond the planet.

Both count. Mix freely.

---



## How 2384 works

The door opens the same year; **how they find it** reflects outlook:


| Outlook                                     | Typical discovery                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Investigative / scientific**              | Subtle exotic physics noticed because they were *looking* for deep rules; phenomena suffice to put them on an interstellar path                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Practical / craft**                       | A material, tool-tradition, or industrial byproduct that *does* hyperspace when used “wrong”                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Ecological / pastoral / less lab-minded** | Travel **falls in their lap** — a species, symbiote, migration, or naturally occurring organ/tool that enables passage with little theory                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Innate-ability**                          | The species already had a natural gift that *can* touch hyperlanes (psionic avatars, brief-dark steps, etc.). **2384** either **opens pathways that had been closed** to that gift, or **reveals a use/aspect** of it they had never understood. Culture and tech need **not** be defined by the gift — though they may be |


Same year. Different epistemology.

## Sol — Humans (*Homo sapiens*)

**Diet:** Omnivore. **Settlement:** Cities and states; residual nomadisms.  
**Plateau:** Iron Age → late medieval; niche steel and local mills; baseline muscle, wind, water.  
**Outlook:** Investigative — builds institutions that chase general rules of nature.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Enduring environments with textiles** — **spinning wheels** (and related machines) drastically cut fabric cost.
2. **Warfare among competing nation-states** — **gunpowder hand-arms**, highly effective in inter-human war and siege.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Work capped by muscle (fed by farms) or by site-tied sources (sail, wheel) that needed convenient energy at point of use.  
- **Disruption:** **Expansion steam engine** — power beyond muscle; chemical fuel moved to where work is.

**2200 — Beyond-as-found:** Permanent Luna / Mars / belt habitats (homeworld still primary).  
**2384 — Door:** **Cherenkov-shear mirrors** in Antarctic ice — light before its neutrino shadow. Noticed because physics culture was already hunting anomalies; math → hyperlanes.  
**Arc:** Conflict and trade leagues; mass literacy → mass media; fragmented polities under orbital law by 2400.  
**First-contact footnote:** Most alien briefings somehow conclude that **Jesus Christ** was a **horologist** (calendar/clock saint, craft-prophet of measured time — the translations compound). Humans correct this; aliens are **surprised but not particularly interested**. The error persists in tourist pamphlets.  
**Fame inversion (Ix-specific):** Soft-Lattice Ix **dig the grooves** of the golden disks — literal tactile interest no other culture shares — and so know **Waldham** (peace message on those disks) far better than Earth does. One of the rare cases where a human figure is more famous among *them* than among humans. Their grudging credit line: “Waldham before Lewis.”

#### Abductee — Moderate — Tidecloth Compact (Thalass)

**Taken:** ~1550. **World:** Warm archipelago; wet rot, naval leagues. **Variant:** Moderate.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Cloth that survives endless wet** — reef-spinners / oil-seal looms.
2. **Island-league war** — coral-powder ship arms for boarding and hull-breach.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Work and war tied to tide and wind schedules; lagoons trap heat uselessly.  
- **Disruption:** **Tide-kettles** — engines that store and release lagoon heat/pressure on demand, freeing labor from the clock of the sea.

**2200:** Atoll habitats on a companion moon.  
**2384:** **Lagoon lensing** (rings focusing starlight) — craft outlook: sailors treat it as a navigational reef-trick before theorists catch up.  
**Arc:** Naval-communal; “drylanders” nickname for Earth.

#### Abductee — Drastic — Rootward Compact (Mycorr)

**Taken:** ~900. **World:** Fungal megacontinent; iron sickens. **Variant:** Drastic.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Feeding dens without plough-metal** — mycelial culture-frames.
2. **Spore-tide predators** — cultivated spore-flash area denial.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Muscle and mycelial growth both stall in cold seasons; no portable fuel tradition.  
- **Disruption:** **Compost-pressure reactors** — living guts that digest waste into continuous work through winter.

**2200:** **Reclamation inversion** — after a continent-scale blight, they re-seed and re-inhabit the dead mycocities rather than fleeing to moons first; Trojan spore-arks come later as insurance.  
**2384:** Cap-walkers notice **lane-cap fruit** — a fungus that fruits only along invisible chords; eating/sporing it moves matter. Ecological lap-fall; theory optional.  
**Arc:** Call Earth metal-poisoners; kinship genetic first.

## First dozen



### 1. Veyri of Keth-Orum

**Diet:** Obligate carnivore (chemistry, not culture — they still need meat-analogs). **Settlement:** Kill-camps → fortress dens →, after the meat-peace, dense dens and pack-cities.  
**Plateau:** ~8 kyr hunting law and corridor war. **Outlook:** Practical-ritual; study is of prey, pack, and later *proxy*.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Preserving kill through lean seasons** — sinew-looms / hide-gins.
2. **Pack war over migration corridors** — blood-glass charges.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Dens could not stack population without starving the range or turning on each other — endless corridor fighting was how surplus aggression and meat-claim got spent. Live prey and pack-war were the same social machine.  
- **Disruption:** **Vat-flesh / synthetic meat** — cultured kill that satisfies obligate chemistry without a carcass. Once stomachs no longer required a hunt, dens could densify. What followed (decades, then custom): **hunt-proxies** — scent-trails, quarry-drones, arena chases, scored corridor-games — substitutes that burn the old instincts without endless real fighting. Social strain of large population density eases; the age of perpetual pack-war ends (feuds remain; genocide-calendar does not).

**2200:** Ice-moon dens and proxy-ranges offworld (abattoir stations become mostly historical / ceremonial). Some **reclamation** of overhunted home ranges as park-proxy wilderness.  
**2384:** Packs singing at Lagrange feel **herd-echoes** — heartbeats syncing across vacuum; they follow the “herd that isn’t there,” now read as the ultimate proxy-quarry. Lap-fall via instinct/ritual, not physics papers.  
**Arc:** From ritual war to proxy sport and trophy media; scent-script elites argue forever whether vat-flesh “counts.” Old packs call the peace soft; most dens call hunger-war barbarism. Brand-Pack cousins will later find all of this **terrifying**.

#### Abductee — Moderate — Second Pack (Coldfold)

**Taken:** ~1200. **World:** Night-side hunting ice. **Variant:** Moderate — same chemistry, harsher calendar.

**1400:** Blubber-reels + frost-charge spears (calories from ice-prey; war on ice bridges).  
**1800:** Constraint = exposure + lean ice; disruption = **frost-vats** (synthetic meat under ice-labs) *and* frost-fat boilers. Proxies catch on slower — ice culture romanticizes “true flense” longer.  
**2200:** Ice-crust shepherd-moon holds; proxy-ranges reluctantly adopted.  
**2384:** **Pack-aurora** — kill-songs light chords; they migrate toward the light as if it were prey-spill.  
**Arc:** Claims “truer” Veyri custom; half-suspicious of parent peace, half glad not to starve.

#### Abductee — Drastic — Brand-Pack (Inkden)

**Taken:** ~1650 into a world where prey was already gone. **World:** Inkden — soft alluvial flats; large prey chemically suppressed or extinct; silt holds marks forever. **Variant:** Drastic — obligate carnivores forced through **mass famine** into agriculture (grain, pulse, engineered flesh-analogs grown as *crops*, not as honored kill).

**Longing:** They carry a deep cultural ache for chase and kill they never lived — songs, scent-myth, corridor-epics, games that *feel* like hunting. It is longing and fantasy, **not** a program to restore hunting as practice. Closer to how humans enjoy violent games while being disturbed if someone acts that out for real — Brand-Pack version dialed harder by famine memory and cult history. The ache stays; the deed stays unthinkable.

**Historical baggage:** Centuries after the famine — once Inkden was tamed and their industrial revolution had touched every corner of the planet — a **blood-purity murder cult** rose in the prosperous dens under a leader called **Marrek Seal-True**: hierarchical, eliminationist, practicing **cannibalism** as “true meat” and hunting of persons as restoration of the lost gift. The ensuing genocide and war killed over 20% of the planetary population before the blood-purity murder cult was defeated.  Entire cultures were not only hunted to extinction but erased, the blood purity cult tried to destroy their languages and all knowledge of them.  The war left such extreme ecological scars upon their planet that some of them persist today.  The homelands of the blood purity murder cult are a radioactive wasteland that they have no interest in reclaiming, it's a cursed place they revile. Historians still split: some blame the **plutocrats** of that industrial peak (decadence, trophy ideology, meat-nostalgia as elite fashion); others blame the **backlash to those plutocrats** (purity politics that ate its own). Ever since, *actually hunting* (or treating fantasy-kill as a model for life) reads as **decadent elite cruelty** at best and **one step from that blood-purity** at worst.  The hunting instinct is a dangerous impulse to be overcome not something to embrace.

**1400:** Bead-ration mills + brand-ink charges — born of famine accounting and soft war over water/soil, not corridor glory.  
**1800:** Constraint = trust and ration at distance while agriculture industrializes; disruption = **seal-pistons** and agricultural engines that make non-hunt calories reliable. Planet-taming accelerates.  
**Post-industrial cult era → aftermath:** The murder cult belongs to the *rich* centuries, not the hungry ones. After its fall: deliberate peace-craft — restorative law, land-care covenants, anti-cult education. Roughly the **past four centuries** of enormous social effort toward **harmony** and **ecological responsibility**. Autotroph peoples (Ix, Ylth, Softnight’s meadows, etc.) are met with **reverence and jealousy**: bodies that eat light without blood.  
**2200:** Mark-moons of public brand walls and soil-archive stations; dens as garden-forts, not abattoirs.  
**2384:** Efforts to reconstruct genomic diversity lost in the blood purity war discover a mathematical formula encoded into the combined genetic materials of extinct people and species.  
**Arc:** When they meet **parent Veyri** — vat-peace, proxy-hunts, dens that still smell like chase — they are **terrified**. Parent proxies and chase-sport feel like someone who loved the violent game and then built a lifestyle around it; parent nostalgia for “real kill” is worse. Brand-Pack diplomats smile, brand carefully, and keep the children home. Parent Veyri find them tragic and over-careful; Brand-Pack find parents a living nightmare with bad intentions.  The worst part for the brand-pack is that they find living among other Veyri cultures enjoyable.

### 3. Drannock Clades

**Diet:** Herbivore. **Settlement:** Rigid farm-hexes.  
**Plateau:** ~25 kyr hex-custom. **Outlook:** Custom-bound; discovery is accidental breach of ritual.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Fair boundaries under population pressure** — field-gins / survey stakes.
2. **Chronic border ritual** — hex-charges for duel-plots.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** A hex could only work what muscle and season allowed inside its lines; surplus couldn’t move without violating custom.  
- **Disruption:** **Plot-boilers** — shared engines legally “owned by the boundary,” letting energy cross hexes without people “trespassing” labor.

**2200:** Hex-moon field stations *and* reclamation of sink-dead hex belts on the homeworld as “new hexes.”  
**2384:** **Missing hexes** appear in surveys — vacant plots that sketch lane graphs. They fence them. Travel begins when someone falls through a fenced absence. Lap-fall via cadastral ritual.  
**Arc:** Border ritual endless; soil-marks as property.

**2384:** Silence wells — places with no echo — treated as cursed hexes, then doors.

#### Abductee — Drastic — Spectacle Hex (Roargrid)

### 5. Hecate Line

**Diet:** Omnivore. **Settlement:** Sparse tower-holds.  
**Homeworld:** Unusually **easy** for them — mild climate bands, reliable forage/small game, few disasters that demand work-gangs. Scarcity rarely forces cities, so **distance etiquette is affordable**.  
**Plateau:** ~4 kyr solitude etiquette built on surplus leisure, not on racial genius or an inability to cooperate. Thin protocols (signals, ciphers, cold diplomacy) exist; dense teamwork is simply seldom *necessary*. Idle time turns into solo craft and private study.  
**Outlook:** Cipher-investigative — private science from spare seasons, shared reluctantly.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Signal without inviting guests** — echo-looms / message cloth (coordination at range, still alone at home).
2. **Trespass by expansionist neighbors** — whisper gonnes (end contact fast; restore distance).

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Even on an easy world, deep winter and tower upkeep still stole the leisure that private study depended on — unless one took retainers, which most refused.  
- **Disruption:** **Solo-boilers** — compact engines sized for a single hold, preserving retainer-free life *and* winter study time.

**2200:** Hermit orbitals (offworld solitude; ease recreated by tech).  
**2384:** **Self-interference fringes** in personal cipher — messages that arrive before they’re sent. Noticed because long leisure already went into auditing their own codes; subtle-physics path, not a species IQ quirk.  
**Arc:** Cold diplomacy; mass media hated; look “sophisticated hermits” to outsiders who miss how much the homeworld was doing for them.

#### Abductee — Moderate — Mirror-Alone (Nullspire)

**Taken:** ~1350. **World:** Sound-eating air; still relatively easy living, just quieter. **Variant:** Moderate.

**1400:** Null-mirror stacks + quiet charges.  
**1800:** Solo-boilers (protect leisure under light-only comms).  
**2200:** Hermit orbitals.  
**2384:** Fringes crystalline; may ignore parent hails.  
**Arc:** Ultra-solitary; same ease-plus-distance pattern.

#### Abductee — Drastic — Crowdwell Seed (Crowdwell)

**Taken:** ~1600. **World:** Toxic flats; forage fails; **only stacks live**. **Variant:** Drastic — hard living strips the surplus that made solitude work.

**1400:** Crowd-gins + tower-seed charges (plague/stack safety; corridor faction war).  
**1800:** Constraint = stacks suffocate without shared pumps; disruption = **stack-boilers** as civic utilities (solitude tech inverted into commons).  
**2200:** **Reclamation** of toxic flats with sealed arcologies *and* crowded moon overflows.  
**2384:** Empty rooms casting two shadows — urban ghost story that engineers eventually instrument. Mixed outlook.  
**Media / justice:** Forced density + corridor law produced a **technocratically engineered legal code** — routine contract enforcement carries extremely subtle built-in antecedents, so disputes often resolve *before* spectacle. Done right, this means **less litigation, not more**. Outsiders usually misread the gap as “they like denser mysteries / stricter cops.” Crowdwell themselves would say many other cultures have **low-quality legal systems**: not too authoritarian, not too individualistic, not too simple — just not built carefully enough to work. Safeguarding society and individual rights is ordinary infrastructure to them, not a cultural obsession. They concede real merit to cultures that achieve **social harmony with little law**; what frustrates them is species that are *not* naturally harmonious and still refuse the common-sense engineering Crowdwell treats as obvious. They mostly live with the distance rather than crusade. Human **cop shows** still look childishly simplistic. Murder mysteries from elsewhere are distasteful when they treat something as serious as murder as slipshod adventure instead of careful process.  They admire the Brand-Pack more then the Brand-Pack admire themselves, they're at least trying.
**Arc:** Federation trauma: same Line, suddenly forced to cooperate densely; not “they finally got smart,” but “the easy world is gone.”

### 6. Ylth Swarm-Kin (conventional branch)

**Diet:** Fungal herbivore. **Settlement:** Mycelial metros.  
**Plateau:** ~3.5 kyr farm mysticism. **Outlook:** Ecological; temples experiment, labs are gardens.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Crop failure vs metro hunger** — fruiting-wheels / culture-frames.
2. **Farm-temple schism-war** — spore-flash.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Growth rate capped by ambient compost heat; winter metros starved of work.  
- **Disruption:** **Compost-boilers** — forced hot compost engines decoupling farm labor from season.

**2200:** Trojan garden arks; also **reclamation** of blighted undercities by re-seeding dead metros.  
**2384:** **Cap-ring lightning** along canopies — temple gardeners walk the rings and arrive elsewhere. Symbiosis/lap-fall more than physics.  
**Arc:** Bloody schisms; cautious abroad.

#### Abductee — Moderate — Sunspill Farms (Glarecap)

**Taken:** ~1280. **World:** Harsh bright; thin fungi; open sky as predator. **Variant:** Moderate — same farm-temple politics, light as the enemy.

**1400:** Shade-gins + glare-flash (multiply cover-cloth and condensate catch; heresy wars over “too much sky”).  
**1800:** Constraint = UV kills culture in hours; disruption = still-fed compost boilers that only run under shadecloth, tying industry to shadow.  
**2200:** **Shade-arks** — permanent shadow-habitats: layered canopies, nightside-facing hulls, and moon-towed umbrellas that cast moving dusk over farm belts. Offworld living is less “colony on rock” than **exported night**. Homeworld work becomes tending the ark’s shadow-track.  
**2384:** Cap-ring lightning only at terminators — holy dusk between ark-shade and true night; gardeners walk the ring where ark-shadow meets star-shadow and arrive elsewhere.  
**Arc:** Light-heresy politics; will offer Earth “shade treaties” before trade treaties.

#### Abductee — Drastic — Rail-Spore (Schedulecap)

**Taken:** ~1750; mycelium won’t metro on a flat dry world. **World:** Schedulecap — continental flats; fungi only in cracks. **Variant:** Drastic — garden kings become clock-priests.  
**Look:** **Clockpunk** — public life is escapements, bell-towers, brass timetable boards, and visible gear-trains; temples are clock halls; spoilage is a sin against the dial.

**1400:** Timetable-gins + rail-charges (move water/starter; junction war).  
**1800:** Constraint = distance vs spoil; disruption = **locomotive boilers** geared into town clocks so departures, harvest windows, and boiler stoking share one beat.  
**2200:** Rail-moon yards — orbital berths hung with synchronized master-clocks; gauge and gear as diplomacy.  
**2384:** Trains that arrive before they leave on certain gauges — schedule priests follow the ghost timetable when a station clock runs *fast toward the stars*.  
**Arc:** Clocks over caps; parents call them dried-out; they answer with a corrected schedule.

### 13. Vael of the Second Skin

**Diet:** Omnivore. **Settlement:** Watershed leagues and ridge towns — cooperative more often than feuding nation-states.  
**Plateau:** ~2.1 kyr of guild and parish law.  
**Innate gift:** Most adults can cast a **second skin** — a short-range psionic avatar that sees, speaks, and (weakly) manipulates within about a kilometer. Skins can also **sheathe** the body in a felt force-layer (weather, modesty, work abrasion), so woven clothing was never a civilizational bottleneck. The gift is treated like a trade skill or sport, not a psi-theocracy; ploughs, water-rights, and quarries matter more.  
**Outlook:** Practical with a quiet mystic minority; the gift is familiar, therefore half-ignored.  
**Vs humans:** Parallel *shape* (cities, industry, later skin-pilots), different *1400 drivers* — fabric-as-power and mountain-breaking hydraulics, not cloth-as-clothing and gunpowder states.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Catching and aiming wind when bodies already “wear” skins** — **sails** and **windmill cloth** (and the frames/gins that cheapen large yardage). Textile boom is about **prime movers and haul**, not garments; Vael jokes that humans invented the wheel of shirts.
2. **Resource desperation under hard overburden** — metals, salts, and deep aquifers locked behind ridges. Not driven by endless nation-state war; driven by shortage. **Hydraulic rams / pressure-mines** — stored water and pump-heads used to crack and strip mountainsides, opening deposits that hand and avatar could not reach.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Pressure work and mills still needed water **at the site** — high valleys and dry belts stayed poor even after the mountain-breaks.  
- **Disruption:** **Closed-loop pressure mains** — high-pressure fluid piped to point of need (a hydraulic cousin to human steam’s “fuel to the work,” grown from their own 1400), with heat-boosters where watersheds fail. Avatars still cannot haul bulk.

**2200:** Moon towns and belt farms; some crews staff hulls with one body and three skins. Also **reclamation** of flood-smashed watershed capitals and worked-out pressure-quarries refitted as cities.  
**2384 — Door (innate unlock):** Every second skin had always hit a hard **horizon wall** beyond which the avatar dissolved. In 2384 the wall **thins along certain bearings** — skins that push through do not die; they step into hyperlane capillaries and can be walked, with training, to other stars. Not a new organ: a newly opened road.  
**Arc:** Embarrassed boom of “skin-pilot” guilds beside still-ordinary watershed politics; humans keep asking if they’re a psychic race; Vael keep answering “we also have pressure mains.” First-contact textile talks are mutually baffling.

#### Abductee — Moderate — Nearskin Compact (Closeglen)

### 14. Orth of the Brief Dark

**Diet:** Omnivore. **Settlement:** Hill forges and terrace farms; clan holds.  
**Plateau:** ~3.4 kyr of smith-custom and terrace law.  
**Innate gift:** Orth can take a **brief dark** — a step of a few body-lengths through an inky interval, emerging elsewhere in line of intent. Used for forge-work (skip the hot side), rescue, petty theft, duel flourish. Most Orth insist it is “just how legs work when you mean it.” Tech path is metallurgy and terrace agriculture, not a blink-cult. Ranged arms never became central: why throw when you can **arrive already inside the guard**? They rarely study the gift deeply — divine generosity taken as weather.  
**Outlook:** Craft-practical; mystics exist and are teased.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Terrace yield on thin soil** — **contour-gins** and seed-drills that multiply food without claiming more hill.
2. **Clan feud over forge-rights** — not gun-lines but the **interval-peen**: a short, murderously balanced melee hammer/axe with a **single-shot cap-face** — a percussion-cap-style energetic puck on the striking surface that detonates once on contact (flash, concussive pop, not an explosion; tactically like smacking someone with a hammer you strapped a cap to). Meant to be swung in the same breath as a brief-dark step — strike from absence, one crack of force, gone before the ward answers. Caps are reloaded like cartridges; duel law is obsessed with cap-truth and reload timing.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Deep ore and long hauls capped muscle and local charcoal; blinks don’t move wagons.  
- **Disruption:** **Furnace-wind engines** — portable blast and haul, decoupling forges from hillside charcoal rights.

**2200:** Forge-moons; **reclamation** of slagged ancestral hills reopened as living terraces.  
**2384 — Door (innate revelation):** The brief dark was always a nick in hyperspace foam. In 2384 Orth navigators (often smiths, not priests) learn to **lengthen and aim** the interval — hold the dark a breath longer, step along a bearing, and surface in another system’s shadow. Pathways weren’t so much locked as **misread** as mere footwork. Brightstep Orth will later call this “noticing the altar was always a door.”  
**Post-2384 kin contact:** When Brightstep (and soon Softnight) re-enter the conversation, parent Orth are **exasperated**. The cousins are just *weird* — sacred twilight cosplay, nap theology, picnic lane policy — and neither lineage ever developed **psychiatry**. Parent Orth didn’t build their whole culture around it; they simply have ordinary mind-healers, grief-counselors, and post-duel care like any sane hill society. That both abductee cultures treat the absence as unremarkable is **disturbing**. Parent diplomats sigh, reload cap-faces, and refer cousins to clinics with the same tired patience they use for Brightstep pamphlets.  
**Arc:** Embarrassed redesign of duel law; “how legs work” becomes the most expensive sentence in Orth diplomacy; kin reunions feel like family therapy they didn’t ask for but probably needed.

#### Abductee — Moderate — Softnight Kin (Milknight)

**Taken:** ~1200. **World:** Milknight — orbital dynamics so messy the calendar is a priesthood of its own: **days and nights last weeks to months**. Night is a land of milk and honey; day is apocalyptically lethal. **Variant:** Moderate gift-range, totally different life-strategy.  
**Look / vibe:** Space hippies — patient, indoor, conflict-soft, zero urgency cosplay.

**Adaptation:** They copied the local biosphere and learned to **hibernate** through the deadly season, holing up in sealed holds. Empire-building became impractical across week-long noons; they got extremely good at **resolving conflict without warfare**. Long indoor stretches with little necessary work grew a culture that is **extremely laid back and extremely patient** — art, talk, slow craft, and **nap theology**.  
**Izzy:** Culture-hero / half-saint of the Long Noon — the (possibly composite) Softnight who first treated sleep through the scourge as *wisdom* rather than weakness. **Nap theology** is formally the **Izzyine rite**: scheduled rests as civic duty, dispute freezes during berth-hours, and the proverb *“Izzy would nap first.”* Hibernation berths are **Izzies**; a polite goodbye is “Izzy keep you.” Brightstep pamphlets spend footnotes raging at Izzy. Softnight find this delightful and do not argue.

**1400 — Needs & tools**

1. **Keeping holds livable across month-noons** — **seal-gins**, air/water recyclers, and hibernation-berth frames (practical study of sleep-metabolism).
2. **Disputes that cannot wait for spring-war** — **accord-looms** and mediation rites that bind settlements without armies; the interval-peen exists as a dance tool more than a murder tool.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption**

- **Constraint:** Hibernation economies couldn’t move bulk or mend holds while the world burned outside.  
- **Disruption:** **Dream-boilers / hold-engines** — stored work through the sleep season so waking nights start already fed and repaired. Still no conquest calendar.

**Later boom (short years, ~late 2100s):** They find a **natural resource** — absurdly convenient — that strips day’s scourge for them *and* the native ecology. In a few short years Milknight is safe year-round. The biosphere has **no natural predators**; meadow animals never evolved to flee. Softnight Orth frolic with creatures that treat them as weather. Brightstep Orth, who bled centuries for sacred twilight science, are **indignant about every single thing** about this: the easy world, the nap empire, the free fix, the friendly ungulates, the cheerful ignorance of entanglement liturgy. Softnight bear **exactly zero animosity** back — they call Brightstep “daysteppers” fondly, offer tea, and do not understand the break-up letters.

**2200:** Year-round meadow habitats and gentle moon pastures; reclamation is just… opening the shutters.  
**2384:** Lane-aiming arrives as a curiosity between naps; they follow Brightstep papers when polite, parent Orth hunches when convenient, and mostly ask whether the destination has good shade for picnics.  
**Arc:** Patient hippie Orth; diplomats who win by outlasting the argument; Brightstep’s Unearned Dusk museum needs a whole Milknight pavilion. No psychiatry tradition — disputes freeze, naps help, and everyone assumes that’s enough. Parent Orth find this alarming. Contingency lore: if Milknight ever fell entirely, their galaxy-wide parting broadcast would be *“To sleep: perchance to dream: ay, there’s the ray.”*

#### Abductee — Drastic — Neverdark (Brightstep)

**Taken:** ~900; brief dark fails in hard daylight. **World:** Brightstep — a rare **close-pair trinary**: two suns in a tight binary (Brightpair) and a distant tertiary (Farstep). The Neverdark homeworld rides a **horseshoe / barycentric** orbit between the pair and Farstep, producing almost no true night and a brutal climate belt: a **frozen ice wall at the equator**, **burning-hot poles**, temperate bands between; the pole facing the Brightpair holds a large habitable temperate zone, while the far pole’s temperate cap is tiny. Pole flip is still a few centuries off. Day **suppresses** the gift. Thin soil. **Variant:** Drastic — centuries without reliable steps.

**1400 — Needs & tools** *(survival without the gift)*

1. **Staying fed and watered under twin-sun drought** — **contour-gins**, deep cisterns, heat-shade cloth as agriculture, not theology.
2. **Clan feud and resource war without blinks** — **crucible-spears** and ward-pikes: deadly melee still, but plant-footed, no interval-peen culture. The lost gift is lament and legend, not battlefield grammar.

**1800 — Constraint & disruption** *(still not “solving” daylight)*

- **Constraint:** Muscle and local charcoal/heat under lethal noon; no brief-dark logistics.  
- **Disruption:** **Furnace-wind engines** and sealed cool-works — ordinary industrial survival on a cruel world. Twilight remains a dream in scripture and fringe papers, not infrastructure.

**The long dream (plateau through ~2100s):** For centuries Brightstep Orth collect every anomaly — eclipse minutes, cave-pockets, storm dimmings, rare births who stepped once at noon — and treat those incidents as **sacred data**. Parent Orth never bothered; Softnight Orth hibernated and later *bought* dawn with a rock. Brightstep theology insists the gift is grace, and that **learning its precise nature** (photon entanglement, exotic-particle coupling, why day forbids the nick) is how a people draw closer to the divine — not mere shade-cloth, not milk-and-honey naps.

**~2100s–2200 — Beyond-as-found:** They finally achieve **artificial twilight** worthy of the dream: engineered spectra and particle-occluding fields that restore brief-dark reliably. Expansion follows usable dusk outward — shade-side moons, twilight belts, **reclamation** of sunglare provinces under sacred occlusion. Labs are temples; equations are liturgy. News of Milknight’s casual year-round fix arrives like a theological war crime.

**2384 — Door:** Their sacred model **forecast** the universal event decades early — papers and temple-calendars from the 2340s–2360s already name a coming “answering season” when entanglement windows would widen. When 2384 arrives on schedule (their schedule), they don’t stumble into lane-aiming; they **execute** predicted twilight/entanglement regimes that lengthen the dark into hyperlane capillaries. Parent Orth call it hindsight cosplay; Softnight offer congratulations and a picnic. Brightstep has the dated liturgy to prove they earned it — and the proof only makes the resentment louder.  
**Causation schism:** A **substantial minority** of Brightstep (“daysteppers”) hold that their answering-season rites and entanglement work didn’t merely *meet* 2384 — they **caused** the universal opening, and every other culture’s hyperlane discovery is downstream of Brightstep’s step into the foam. **Most** Brightstep reject full causation but grant it is **theoretically possible** given how their model couples local twilight fields to wider foam; they call the minority claim a **rather substantial logical leap**. Some scientists elsewhere (Orth and non-Orth) quietly agree: possible on paper, not nearly proven. Parent Orth’s vernacular dismissal — *“Legs don’t step that far, child!”* — becomes interstellar shorthand for the leap. Softnight nod politely and ask if anyone needs a nap.  
**Arc:** Most scientific *and* most devout Orth culture. Toward parent Orth: industrial-grade seething (sermons, satire, tariff-sermons, conversion-gated marriage, Unearned Dusk museums, break-up diplomacy). Toward Softnight/Milknight: **same voltage, different flavor** — apoplectic at the hibernation laziness, the predatorless frolic, the easy natural-resource dawn, and above all the fact that Softnight smile through every pamphlet and bear **zero animosity**. Softnight will trade and even fight *beside* Brightstep against outsiders while calling them daysteppers and asking if they’ve eaten. Brightstep will not forgive grace shrugged *or* grace napped through. Pamphlets are weapons. Smugness historically justified; warmth not included. Brightstep likewise never developed psychiatry — sacred twilight and equations, no mind-healers; parent Orth refer cousins to clinics with exasperated charity.

## Aside — Nomad freighters (ark-sleepers)

One of the **nomad** peoples is not a home-cluster empire: they slept through the
**Long Night** on **ark ships** in interstellar space and woke with everyone else
in **2384**. While conventional empires still have **locked beltways**, these
nomads sell **cut-throat shipping** between home clusters — cheap lanes before
the ring network is open.

**Speech tic:** In every pitch, plea, or toast they insist *“I’ve got **N** kids
to feed!”* (or local idiom). **N** is always tailored to the listener’s species:
a **large but still plausible** claim for *that* people — never a random joke
number, never the nomads’ own brood count. For solitary / small-family cultures
that means a big nuclear household; for **spore, mycelial, hive, and crust-colony**
peoples it means a **collective parental claim** (many adults jointly “own”
hundreds or thousands of young). To humans: **“I’ve got 8 kids to feed!”**

precision. Nullspire find it invasive *and* innumerate; everyone else files it
under freighter folklore.

(to an Ix) is spoken like an invoice line, as if they audited someone else’s nursery.

**Nomads among themselves** still tailor N to whoever is in the room; they do not
use a fixed “true” count in public speech.

### Hinge checklist


| Hinge    | Rule of thumb                                                                                                       |

### Settled design notes (from review)

- **Shared door, disagreed ontology:** Two cultures **can** share a door mechanism and still disagree on whether it is “physics” or “livestock” (or craft accident, gift, omen, etc.). First contact arguments about *what the door is* are features.
- **Drastic abductee 2384 outlook:** Convergence is **allowed** — a drastic isolate may rediscover something like the parent’s epistemology (species memory, parallel reasoning, or contact), not only the new world’s outlook.
- **Innate-ability 2384:** Something **universal** happened in 2384, but it may have **manifested in extremely different ways** (opened walls, taught aiming, seasonal flicker, eclipse-stacked revelation, etc.). Local cultures can mistake their manifestation for the whole event.



### Still fuzzy

- **Reclamation-at-2200 vs 2384 attention:** No hard rule. A culture deep in homeworld rebuild might notice the door later, earlier, or on the same calendar beat as everyone else — pick per story.



### Abductees

15 homeworlds × (1 moderate + 1 drastic); Taken before ~1800.

## One-line summary

Fifteen homeworlds sync on four dates: ~1400 answers two urgent needs with tools; ~1800 breaks a named constraint with a disruptive technology; ~2200 lives beyond the homeworld-as-found (colonies and/or reclamation); **2384** opens hyperspace via deep-rule hunts, craft accidents, lap-fall ecology, or — for some — **unlocking/reframing a gift they already had** — while moderate and drastic abductees remix those paths under other skies.
