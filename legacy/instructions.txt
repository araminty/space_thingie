I was you to sketch out a prototype of a starmap for a hypothetical space 4X game.  I'm going to progressively lay out details of the mapmaking progress so for now just write a simply python script (using the wsl python) which will show the positions of the stars that that will be in the game.  The final version will have a couple thousand stars but for now let's just focus on a small section of the galaxy, perhaps a hundred stars.  The stars should be connected by "lanes" between stars that are within a radius that makes them "adjacent" but have a condition to prevent lanes between two stars that cut too close to a third star.  The distance that counts as adjacent should be scaled to the density of stars in the neighborhood.  There should also be a fallback to make sure that every star is connected to the network.



Ideally the starmap should be done so that stars have varying "heights" but they aren't too close to directly above or below each other so that the map can be viewed from a perspective above without too much difficulty.



We want the stars to be roughly evenly spread out through the area.  There should still be empty areas and variation, just not most of the space as gaps between star clusters.



A 3D visualization with a tool such as bokeh (I dunno if that's actually the best choice) would be good.

PROGRESSIVE UNLOCKING

---



Goal: the galaxy map is revealed in stages. Locked stars stay visible on the map

but use a distinct color. Travel is only allowed along unlocked lanes.



Prototype scale: ~2000 stars outside the galactic core (core is extra on top).



Star tiers:



1) Home clusters (~20% of all stars) — color: blue

   - Several compact pockets of stars scattered around the outer galaxy.

   - Cluster count scales with galaxy size; each cluster covers roughly ~2% of

     the galaxy's area, so ~10 clusters when targeting ~20% of stars.

   - No home-cluster stars in the galactic center.

   - Home clusters may sit anywhere in the outer disk (near the rim or closer

     to the core boundary) — not confined to a mid-radius band that leaves only

     frontier at the edge and around the core.

   - Stars inside a home cluster are fully interconnected among themselves

     (lanes within the cluster do not depend on the ring network below).

   - At game start, ONLY intra-home-cluster lanes are traversable (players are

     bottled up inside their starting cluster).



2) Ring network (connective "beltway") — color: white

   - Thin tendrils looping around the galaxy (parallel trunks ~4–5 star spacings

     apart), not a thick filled ring band.

   - Links home clusters for later mid-game travel.

   - May pass through a home cluster or only touch its edge; stars on a tendril

     that cut through a home cluster are beltway (white), not home. Remaining

     home stars connect to those beltway stars with home-colored (blue) links.

   - Home clusters remain internally connected even without the ring.

   - Ring stars/lanes start LOCKED / untraversable.



3) Locked frontier (remaining stars) — color: red

   - Visible but not yet traversable.

   - They may have geometric lanes to other stars, but those lanes are forbidden

     until a later unlock stage.

   - In particular, any lane between a home-cluster star and a locked-frontier

     star is treated as locked for now.



Viewer colors for now: home=blue, ring=white, frontier=red.

NOMADS AND ANCIENTS

---



Nomads: do not affect map generation for now. They are entities that can travel

the ring network/beltway from the start (unlike players bottled in home clusters).



Ancients:

- Four Ancients, each with its own color (gold / purple / green / orange in the viewer).

- Galaxy is divided into 12 equal slices. Pattern around the disk:

  Ancient0 owns 2 slices, then 1 empty, Ancient1 owns 2, empty, Ancient2 owns 2,

  empty, Ancient3 owns 2, empty.

- Each Ancient has clusters spread across its slices (one cluster per owned slice).

- Every Ancient cluster has:

  - an occupied core of ~0.5% of the galaxy's stars

  - a periphery of unoccupied stars of about the same count (home-cluster blue)

- Ancient occupied stars render as a semitransparent blue sphere with the Ancient

  color as a smaller nucleus at half the blue sphere's radius. All stars are

  drawn semitransparent so this nested coloration reads clearly.

- Intra-Ancient-cluster lanes (core + periphery) use the same unlock tier as

  intra-home-cluster lanes (traversable at start).

- The ring network always attaches to each Ancient cluster at a periphery star.

- Same-Ancient green beltway chains are complete paths: from the periphery star

  beside an occupied Ancient star, along the beltway, to the periphery star

  beside an occupied star in another cluster of that same Ancient.
