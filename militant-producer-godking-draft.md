# Draft — Post-abduction god-monarch trauma → militant producers

Working notes only. Goal: find a **less-fleshed** conventional lineage whose **parent** can become **extreme War + extreme Produce**, while **both abductees leave before the trauma** and so do **not** share the militancy (or the producer ideology born from it).

Not committed to `conventional-species-history-c.md` yet.

---

## Pattern (shared)

### Required beats

1. **Both abductees Taken** (moderate + drastic) while the homeworld is still “ordinary” for that species — plateau politics, no sky-tyrants.
2. **After the later Taken**, a **dangerous alien species** crash-lands (ship, seed-ark, wounded warfleet — pick per version).
3. They rule as **cruel god-monarchs** for **millennia** (not a short occupation). Local religion, law, and food systems warp around them.
4. A **rebellion** eventually **wipes out the god-monarch polity** on that world — thrones, priesthoods, and local bloodlines of the overlords **end**. Timing: **done by ~1400 at latest**. The **species** is not galaxy-extinct: **another culture of the same people** still knocks around as **nomads** (fleets, ark-caravans, lane-scavengers — not planetary emperors).
5. A short **liberation sprint** (decades): reclaim native language, then basic science and/or extremely sophisticated crafts under deadline pressure — so the parent can hit the **shared** ~1400 hinge with everyone else.
6. The **parent culture’s** long aftermath: **militant** (organized war as civic virtue / never-again doctrine) **and producer** (grow/tend/photosynthesize — refuse the overlords’ predation economy). Trait target: **War @ extreme**, **Produce @ extreme**, third dial free. Later hinges (~1800 / ~2200 / 2384) stay on the common calendar.
7. **Abductees** develop on other worlds with **no god-kings**. They are the **untransformed** continuity of the pre-crash parent culture: fold today’s soft/parenthetical parent beats into *them* so the homeworld has room for occupation → sprint → militant Produce. Contact is asymmetrical: parents assume cousins share the trauma; cousins find parents terrifying zealots — and parents may find cousins naively “pre-god” or god-shaped.
8. **Post-2384 landmine:** when the parent meets the **nomad kin** of their dead gods, recognition is visceral (scent, silhouette, tech-grammar, old throne-words in freighter cant). Nomads may not know the god-Hex history — or may tell a very different story (“exiles,” “the lost court,” “the herd that killed its shepherds”).

### Liberation sprint (post-rebellion catch-up)

The god-age does **not** leave a ready medieval toolkit. Typical parental memory: throne-cant, tribute skills, fear. The sprint is how they still make ~1400 with everyone else — **when liberation is on time**.


| Phase (sketch, ~years)   | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **0–5**                  | Thrones fall; mass graves of gods; emergency food law (Produce doctrine hardens under famine risk).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **5–20**                 | **Language reclaim** — old hex/song/knot speech dug out of rural pockets, outlaw graffiti, and pre-crash inscriptions; throne-cant banned or quarantined as curse-tongue.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **15–40**                | **Craft/science bloom** — either a crash course in measurement and “why the world works” *or* hypertrophied practical arts (fort-gins, soil chemistry, anti-cavalry geometry) that look like genius to outsiders who don’t know the deadline. Uneven is fine: brilliant field engineering beside shaky abstract theory, or the reverse. |
| **~1400 hinge**          | Two breakthrough tools land **on schedule**, answering post-liberation needs (defense of gardens, fair plots without god-brands, portable grow-power, etc.).                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **~1800 / ~2200 / 2384** | Same shared hinges; doctrine has had centuries to institutionalize, but the *calendar* never lagged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |


**Design note:** Outsiders (and abductee cousins) may later call this “impossible.” Parents answer: *we had to*, or *the soil remembered*. Do not require a second delayed industrial timeline **for on-time liberators**.

### Discovery under the boot — obsessive bloom despite repression

The craft/science bloom is **not only** a post-liberation crash course. Even under **extremely brutal** god-monarch repression, occupied peoples can develop an **obsessive trend of discovery** that the thrones cannot fully stamp out.


| Piece                       | Intent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pressure**                | Tribute, famine, and sacred bans make curiosity dangerous — and therefore *precious*. Smuggled measurement, illegal soil trials, hidden grammar of pre-crash speech.                                                                    |
| **The ~1400 deadline**      | In-setting or meta: the same mysterious synchrony that pulls other worlds toward ~1400 also pulls *them*. Occupied cultures feel a historical weather change — “the century is arriving” — and invent anyway, in cellars and hedgerows. |
| **Thrones fail to stop it** | Gods outlaw books, break instruments, kill teachers; the obsession mutates (oral ciphers, craft-only “theories,” garden plots as labs). Repression **selects for** stubborn discoverers.                                                |
| **At liberation**           | The sprint is less “reinvent everything from zero” and more **surface and weaponize** what went underground — language reclaim + legalizing the hidden bloom + aiming it at fort-gardens and never-again war.                           |
| **Tone**                    | Tragic and proud: they were becoming a ~1400 people *while cattle*. The rebellion rides a wave that was already cresting.                                                                                                               |


This layer is compatible with on-time liberators (bloom under boot → explode at freedom → still hit ~1400) **and** with the late-liberator exception below.

### Contact irony (optional, all versions)

- Parents brief the galaxy: “We ended gods. Do not bring kings.”
- Abductees: “What gods?”
- Predators (Veyri, Khar, etc.) trigger parental threat displays even when polite.
- True autotrophs (Ix) may be greeted as *holy allies* or *insufficiently armed gardeners*, depending on version.
- **Nomad god-kin:** parents go weapons-hot or theological-crisis; nomads try to sell passage and insist *“I’ve got N kids to feed!”* (or their own tic) while sitting in the silhouette of dead divinities. Diplomats elsewhere call this “the awkward freighter problem.”

### Origin of the god-monarchs — **woke ugly / woke early** (**locked lean**)

The thrones are not a random predator empire from next door. They are **ark-sleepers of their species who woke too early**.


| Piece               | Intent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Long Night arks** | Same broad phenomenon as other nomad ark-sleepers (see draft C aside) — sleep through interstellar dark, wake into history.                                                                                                                            |
| **Early wake**      | A ship / clutch / court thaws **centuries or millennia before 2384**, stranded, hungry, and coherent enough to found a religion of itself.                                                                                                             |
| **Woke ugly**       | Early waking selects for (or creates) cruelty: unfinished minds, famine ethics, god-delusion, broken crew hierarchy turning into monarchy. “Ugly” is moral *and* civilizational — they arrive as a nightmare version of what on-time awakeners become. |
| **On-time kin**     | Other arks of the **same people** wake with everyone else in **2384** (or stay mobile without ever ruling a world) → the **living nomads**. Same silhouette; different century; different sins.                                                        |
| **Recurrence**      | Early-wake ugly landings can happen **more than once**, on **more than one** world. Each is a local god-age; not one galactic empire.                                                                                                                  |


**Contact punchline:** scarred producers meet polite 2384 nomads and smell the throne anyway. Nomads meet scarred producers and hear “you are the hunger that ruled us” — often for the first time.

## Recurring early-wake: which **moderate** abductees could go extreme?

If the pattern repeats, a **moderate** abductee (normally the “mild remix” of the parent) can instead take an early-wake occupation and become an **extreme** War+Produce (or adjacent extreme) culture — while their **parent** and/or **drastic** sibling stay on other paths.

### Selection criteria

Good candidates are moderate abductees that are:

1. **Thin enough** to absorb a millennium + liberation sprint without smashing a jewel-box arc.
2. **Able to land Produce** (or already Produce-adjacent) so “militant gardener” reads honest.
3. **Currently gentle / mild** in tone — so the turn to extreme War is a real transformation.
4. **Taken early enough** for a crash + millennia + rebellion-by-~1400 on *their* world.
5. Ideally leave at least one relative (**parent** or **drastic**) as the **untransformed** mirror.

## Inverse arc — **pacifying god-monarchs** → religious peaceniks

Not all early-wake ugliness is livestock tyranny. Some courts wake into **peace-absolutism**: silence-theocracy, no-banner law, sacred nonviolence enforced by atrocity, “the only hymn is stillness.” Survivors after liberation (or converts who *keep* the peace after killing the gods) can become **religious peaceniks** — War↓ / Peace@, often Hide or Sole, sometimes Produce if the gods forbade blood-meals.

This is the foil to militant gardeners: same species hazard class (early-wake ark-kin), **opposite doctrine**.

### Cultural touchstones — the two shouts

| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

Wells as vertical worlds. Gods nest in deep ledges, demand dive-tithes (catch, corpses, hostages).

### Parent arc

- Rebellion seals god-ledges; rim towns become fortress-gardens; dive becomes **patrol**, not only harvest.
- Biology may stay omnivore; culture goes **Produce-first** (ledge farms, kelp) and **Predate = god-work**.
- Abductees: Shallow-Tone / Dry-Chime never knew deep thrones.

### Fit

Atmospheric; overlaps Khar/Veyri predation themes less cleanly than Drannock herbivores-as-cattle.
