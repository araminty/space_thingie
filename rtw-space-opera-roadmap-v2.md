## Reintroduce (still current thinking)

- Design thesis: Protection vs Mobility, convex costs, table-flip eras, scavengers, cede-the-ground.
- Hull economy (Protection lock, Reaction, damage slows Mobility / wounded birds).
- Rare void weather (tides, boarding climate).
- **Zero micromanagement as a requirement:** the player may be absent; doctrine + loadout + flags resolve the fight. Standing-order *families* (engagement, fog, skirmish, escape, theater) are still the right knobs.

### Honesty tax (from the old tax stub)

- The **job:** a superior force still sweats; 2–4 bad answers; uncertainty, not a delete button.
- Evaluation checklist (superior sweats, weaker can force a decision, no hard-counter loop).
- Committed layer: **fog**, **fighter volume control**, **blind-fire escape** — as situation tactics and Search/Contact flags (clutter, lock, C2), not as extra states.
- Strike endurance (drop tanks, cooldown after the pulse, CAP-away, intercept fork) is the same job on the side rung.
